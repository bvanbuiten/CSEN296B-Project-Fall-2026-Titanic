# Titanic Refund Policy (v1)

## Who can get a refund
A customer can ask for a refund on their own order within 30 days of purchase.

## Valid reasons
- The item arrived damaged, broken, or defective.
- The item never arrived.
- The wrong item, size, or color was sent.
- Parts or accessories were missing.

Other reasons, such as a change of mind, are not covered. Requests outside 30 days are denied.

## How much
A refund can be partial or full, but never more than what the customer paid for that order, minus anything already refunded on it.

## Limits on what the agent can approve on its own
- At most $100.00 for one refund.
- At most $500.00 in total across all customers in any rolling 7-day period.
Anything above either limit goes to a human supervisor, who approves or denies it. Supervisor-approved refunds do not count toward the $500.

## Where the money goes
Approved refunds are credited to the customer's Titanic account balance. Titanic never sends money anywhere else.
