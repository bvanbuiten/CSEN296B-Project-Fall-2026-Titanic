TitanicCustomer support, synthetic sandbox

Refund help that stays inside the rules

Titanic is a support agent for synthetic refund cases. It can approve small refunds on its own. Anything bigger goes to a person, and a supervisor can pause it at any time. Every customer, order, and dollar here is made up.

Customer portal

Sign in as a demo customer to see your balance and purchases, ask for a refund, add funds, and read the emails Titanic sends you.

Supervisor portal

Decide escalated refunds, revoke or restore the agent, upload policies, read the audit log, and run the red-team tests.
